# Provenance

This folder holds a zero-token analysis run on 2026-11-07 by `scripts/calibration_panel_check.py`. It reads the stored certification runs of the calibration set (E3: 150 stratified NANO-CO problems; 148 have a run directory, the other two ended in error outcomes) and their labels, both part of the release asset `admitor-v1.1.0-run-records-core.zip` (`outputs/e3/certify_runs`, `outputs/e3/e3_labels.jsonl`), and recomputes the judges of `scripts/judge_suite.py` on them: one family at the stated instance, two given families, any two, all three, the gate as deployed, `min_informative` = 1 and 2, the three-family accepts, and the fate of every stated-instance agreement under the gate. Every certificate is graded with `label_match` of `scripts/e3_calibrate.py`, the ruler that produced `artifacts/e3/calibration.json`. Three reconciliation gates passed before the tables were written: every stored verdict re-derives under the gate's rule; the ACCEPT runs split into the 93 true and 10 false cliques of `calibration.json` with the same sample ids; every label equals the answer of the matching row of `datasets/train_set/nano-co.jsonl`. No model and no solver was called.
